About

I'm a full-stack developer who prefers shipping working software over polishing a slide about it. Most of my time goes into products I've built and maintained from the ground up: an accounting platform used for real bookkeeping, an AI-assisted farm management system, and websites like PeakInsights.

I care more about correctness under real usage than about how a feature looks in a demo — the projects in the work section include as much debugging and reconciliation work as new feature development, because that's what keeps software trustworthy once people depend on it.

I work in small, targeted changes rather than sweeping rewrites, and I'd rather hand over one correctly-fixed file than a large diff that's hard to review.
